How to read the line

  1. Each stop is a stage, from seeing a profile to swapping numbers. The train carries 1,000 profiles from the top.
  2. Amber branches show how many get off at each stop. Purple ones lead to Ghost Town, where chats go quiet.
  3. Tap a stop to see why people get off there, or ride the whole line and stop at each one
