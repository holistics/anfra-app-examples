How these numbers are defined

Every number on this page is a metric defined once in datasets/unit_economics.dataset.aml. Open Inspect to trace any chart to its AQL and SQL.
Revenue kept
Amount collected minus refunds. Coupon discounts and failed card charges never count.
LLM cost
Input and output tokens × the model's list price per million tokens.
Payment fees
Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 per successful charge. Stripe keeps it when you refund, so it still counts.
Gross margin
Revenue kept − LLM cost − payment fees. Hosting and salaries are not included.
Revenue timing
Each invoice is spread over the days it pays for, so a month's revenue lines up with that month's AI usage. Billed on Aug 20 means paid for Aug 20 to Sep 19.
Losing you money
A customer who paid something this month and whose gross margin is below zero. Accounts that paid nothing (comped, refunded, failed card) are flagged separately.
