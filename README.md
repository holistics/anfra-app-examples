# Anfra App Examples

Apps built by the Holistics team for the Anfra App Competition. Each app lives in its own folder.

See them live: [anfra.ai/data-apps](https://anfra.ai/data-apps)

## Apps

Every app lives in its own folder under `projects/`.

| App | Idea | Demo | Author |
|---|---|---|---|
| [football-predictions](projects/football-predictions) | Predicts football results and shows the reasons behind each call | [Demo](https://football-predictions.pages.holistics.dev/apps/football-predictions/1-matches) | Khai To |
| [leave-now-or-wait](projects/leave-now-or-wait) | Tells a Ho Chi Minh City commuter whether to leave now or wait out the flooding, with a Mayor-mode what-if simulator | [Demo](https://leave-now-or-wait.pages.holistics.dev/apps/flood) | Khue Pham |
| [galaxy-orbit](projects/galaxy-orbit) | Mobile app publisher as a living galaxy: spot the app that needs you, then plan its growth | [Demo](https://galaxy-orbit.pages.holistics.dev/) | Tri Duong |
| [ran](projects/ran) | A running coach on your Garmin data: how your runs went, how training is going, and whether you can reach your goal race | [Demo](https://ran-demo.pages.holistics.dev) | Tai Nguyen |
| [interaction-patterns](projects/interaction-patterns) | Names and demos analytical interactions for Data Apps, with live examples, AQL, SDK wiring and prompts to copy | [Demo](https://anfra.ai/interaction-patterns/) | Chinh (lelouvincx) |
| [hollydeals](projects/hollydeals) | Turns the weekly sales report into a clickable funnel: what changed this week, where every deal is, and the story behind each one | [Demo](https://hollydeals-demo.pages.holistics.dev/apps/sales-funnel) | Vy Huynh |
| [store-performance](projects/store-performance) | An online store's dashboard that flags unusual weeks in revenue, orders and customers, then shows where the change happened | [Demo](https://store-performance-live.pages.holistics.dev/apps/store-performance) | Vy Huynh |
| [eta-control-tower](projects/eta-control-tower) | A freight control tower: which shipments will miss their promised date, how much value is at risk, and why | [Demo](https://eta-tracking-live.pages.holistics.dev/apps/eta-control-tower) | Vy Huynh |
| [underworld](projects/underworld) | You work the identity desk at Charon's ferry: resolve each soul's claim against the Books, where every verdict is a live query | [Demo](https://anfra-amyra.pages.holistics.dev/apps/nether-v4/realm) | Amyra Nguyen |
| [kumquat-kite](projects/kumquat-kite) | One record for every job, from the first conversation to the acceptance Finance verifies | [Demo](https://kumquat-kite-poc-demo.pages.holistics.dev/#/welcome) | Toan Pham |
| [perfume-maker](projects/perfume-maker) | Be a perfumer: mix your own formula, watch the cost per bottle, then price it to make profit | [Demo](https://anfra-uwu.pages.holistics.dev/apps/composer) | Kaia Nguyen |
| [an-eat-het](projects/an-eat-het) | Run a kitchen that tracks menu health, revenue, guests and every kg of food from farm to plate, to make better calls for the kitchen and the planet | [Demo](https://anfra-uwu.pages.holistics.dev/apps/kitchen) | Kaia Nguyen |
| [metric-log](projects/metric-log) | A plugin for Metric Tree & Decompose that keeps a running history for each metric: decisions logged next to it, and every definition change in plain language | [Demo](https://anfra-metric-log-demo.pages.holistics.dev/) | Kaia Nguyen |
| [mouka](projects/mouka) | A personal-finance tracker: where the money went, what's coming up, how the investments are doing and where net worth is heading, every number traceable to its metric | [Demo](https://mouka.pages.holistics.dev/apps/mouka/app) | Nhi Tran |
| [diabetes-visit-prep](projects/diabetes-visit-prep) | Prepares a diabetes clinic visit: the patient's body colored by clinical status, two years as a time-lapse, and a drill into the cause | [Demo](https://diabetes-visit-prep.pages.holistics.dev/apps/diabetes-visit-prep) | Quan Nguyen |
| [library](projects/library) | Walk a book marketplace's catalogue as a low-poly 3D library: rooms sized by revenue, shelves filled by genre, and a book that opens into a spread with a price what-if | [Demo](https://anfra-library.pages.holistics.dev) | Zap |
| [white-space-globe](projects/white-space-globe) | A 3D globe of a SaaS company's customers against each market's size: where it is strong, where the untapped revenue is, and what stands in the way | [Demo](https://anfra-heidi.pages.holistics.dev/apps/DIY/coverage-globe) | Di Hoang |
| [hiring-board](projects/hiring-board) | Every hiring question on one page: where each candidate is, where good candidates come from, which interview steps are slow, and whether headcount lands on plan | [Demo](https://anfra-heidi.pages.holistics.dev/apps/DIY/hiring-board) | Di Hoang |
| [love-line](projects/love-line) | A dating app's funnel as a metro line: of every 1,000 profiles members see, how many lead to a date, and why riders get off at each stop | [Demo](https://anfra-heidi.pages.holistics.dev/apps/DIY/love-line) | Di Hoang |
| [holynews](projects/holynews) | A company's Slack as a newspaper: what everyone applauded, what is heating up, what blew over, and how the office feels | [Demo](https://anfra-heidi.pages.holistics.dev/apps/DIY/holynews) | Di Hoang |
| [ai-margin-radar](projects/ai-margin-radar) | Finds the customers whose AI usage costs more than they pay, and tests the pricing rules that fix the margin | [Demo](https://ai-margin-radar.pages.holistics.dev/apps/ai-margin-radar) |  |
| [churn-radar](projects/churn-radar) | Flags the customers likely to leave and why, then ranks the best action to keep each one | [Demo](https://ai-margin-radar.pages.holistics.dev/apps/churn-radar) |  |
| [usage-analytics](projects/usage-analytics) | A plugin for admins that shows which dashboards and metrics get used, which can be retired, and who has gone quiet | [Demo](https://thang-lk.pages.holistics.dev/apps/usage-xray) | Thang Kim |

Apps ship without data source credentials, so some need setup before they run. Each app README says which sample data it uses.
