# Weather field recordings

- `rain-field.ogg`: **Rain.ogg**, recorded by **ジダネ**, 4 April 2007. Released worldwide into the public domain by the recording's author. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain.ogg — original https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3d/Rain.ogg . Unmodified source; short passages are crossfaded at runtime.
- `thunder-roll.wav`: derivative of the preceding public-domain recording, trimmed from 1.8 to 9.6 seconds, filtered at 35–2600 Hz and faded at both ends. Played after a distance-dependent delay following a rendered lightning strike.

The lyre score is unchanged. These are locally bundled recordings, not external streams.
