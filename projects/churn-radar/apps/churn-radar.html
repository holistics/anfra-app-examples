    How these numbers are defined

    Every number is a metric defined once in datasets/retention.dataset.aml and models/customer_health.model.aml. Open Inspect to see the AQL and SQL behind any chart.
    Churn
    Paid plans cancelled in the month, divided by paying customers that month.
    Revenue lost
    The monthly price customers stopped paying when they cancelled or downgraded. "Lost to the rules" when they named the Agent cap, Pro having no cap, or chat quality.
    Agent runs refused
    Agent mode is the expensive feature (a multi-step task on the Frontier model). Since 1 Oct, Team accounts get 50 runs a month; every attempt after that is refused and counted here.
    AI cost saved
    Agent runs refused by the cap times the average cost of an Agent run, plus chat tokens served by Mini priced at the Standard minus Mini list price.
    Save plays
    Expected revenue kept = (their price + pay-per-run margin) × the chance the play keeps them, minus what the play costs. The chances are your assumptions.
    Actions
    In this demo, Apply and the copilot only simulate the emails and billing changes shown in Activity; nothing leaves the page.
    Risk score
    0-100: usage over the last 28 days against the customer's own normal (up to 45), Agent runs refused by the cap (up to 30), days since last active (up to 15), a failed payment (10). High at 45+, Watch at 30+.
