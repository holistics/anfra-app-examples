What it is

One set tasting menu of 12 to 13 courses that changes with the season. This page follows three years of it: the guests, their drinks, and every kilo of food the kitchen loses.

Why it exists

Knowing where food goes helps us keep more of it in use. This is an ongoing attempt, and being informed by data helps us shape the menu.

Seasonal and local

Food that is in season and grown nearby travels less, needs less cold storage and spoils less. That is why the menu changes four times a year, and why sustainability risk counts how out of season a course is.

How to read it

Menu health scores a course on margin, guest rating and low waste. The food cycle follows each kilo from the farm to where it ends up: reused, composted, fed to animals or thrown away.

The data is synthetic, built to behave like a real kitchen. The fruit season calendar is compiled from Vietnam's fruit seasons.
