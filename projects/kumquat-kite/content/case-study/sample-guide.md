# TP Production Studio: synthetic example

TP Production Studio is a fictional business used to demonstrate Kumquat Kite. No customer discovery material or customer statistics are included.

## Try the workflow

- Choose a demo persona.
- Open a sample project in Job 360.
- As Account, propose a cost change with sample evidence.
- As Finance, review the proposal and its scenario margin.
- Approve it and compare the committed figures with their history.

All amounts, names and records are synthetic. Demo personas are not production authentication.
