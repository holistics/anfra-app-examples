Runalyze's calculations on Banister TRIMP, computed from your per-second heart rate (Garmin's load is kept only for comparison). ATL/CTL = 7/42-day exponentially weighted TRIMP, TSB = CTL − ATL. Monotony = 7-day mean ÷ standard deviation of daily load. Marathon shape = ⅔ weekly-km score + ⅓ long-run (13 km+) score.
